High-Stakes Emergency Hazards & Red Lines Matrix

Critical procedural contraindications and toxicological safety algorithms

Digoxin "Stone Heart" & Calcium Rule

NEVER administer IV Calcium Boluses in acute Digoxin severe toxicity with hyperkalemia. Rapid intracellular calcium overload causes tetanic cardiac contraction ("Stone Heart"). Use Digoxin-specific Fab fragments (Digibind) first.

Spontaneous Coronary Dissection (SCAD)

Avoid PCI stenting in SCAD unless there is ongoing catastrophic ischemia or left main collapse. Stenting carries an extreme risk of propagating hematoma both proximally and distally. Medical therapy is preferred.

TAVR Virtual Transcatheter Distance (VTC)

A Virtual Valve-to-Coronary (VTC) distance < 4mm on pre-procedural CT predicts acute coronary ostial occlusion during TAVR deployment. Wire and protect coronaries prior to valve expansion.

Local Anesthesia Toxicity (LAST) Protocol

In refractory ventricular arrhythmias or collapse post-local anesthetic exposure: Immediately give 20% Lipid Emulsion (1.5 mL/kg IV bolus over 1 min, followed by 0.25 mL/kg/min infusion). Avoid Vasopressin and high-dose Epinephrine.

Heparin-Induced Thrombocytopenia (HIT) Emergency Shift

If platelet count drops >50% post-heparin or acute thrombosis develops: Immediately cease ALL heparin formulations (including flush solutions). Switch to direct thrombin inhibitors: Argatroban (2 mcg/kg/min) or Bivalirudin (0.75 mg/kg bolus, 1.75 mg/kg/h infusion for PCI).
