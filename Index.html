Acute Toxicology & Antidote Matrix

Beta-Blocker Toxicity

1st Line: High-Dose Insulin Euglycemia (HIET) 1 U/kg bolus + 1-10 U/kg/hr drip.

Rescue: Glucagon 5-10mg IV slow push, lipid emulsion therapy 20%.

Calcium Channel Blocker Overdose

1st Line: Calcium Chloride 10% 1-2g IV, HIET infusion, Norepinephrine.

Rescue: VA-ECMO escalation if unresponsive to refractory vasoplegia.

Digoxin Toxicity

1st Line: Digoxin Immune Fab (Fab fragments). Dosing based on serum level.

Hazard: Avoid empiric calcium administration if digoxin toxicity is suspected.

Local Anesthetic Toxicity (LAST)

1st Line: 20% Intralipid 1.5 mL/kg IV bolus over 1 min, followed by 0.25 mL/kg/min infusion.

Avoid: Vasopressin, CCBs, beta blockers, and high-dose local anesthetics.

Mechanical Circulatory Support (MCS) Emergency Safeguards

Impella Suction / Dislodgement

Reduce P-level to P2, check echo for inlet position, adjust position under fluoroscopy or TTE.

VA-ECMO Harlequin Syndrome

Monitor right radial arterial oxygenation. Convert to V-AV ECMO if upper extremity hypoxia occurs.

IABP Gas Leak Alert

Stop pumping immediately. Place patient in Trendelenburg position. Clamp catheter line to prevent air embolus.

High-Risk Hemostasis & Bleeding Reversal

Unfractionated Heparin Overdose Protamine Sulfate (1 mg per 100 U Heparin)
Direct Oral Anticoagulants (DOACs - Apixaban/Rivaroxaban) Andexanet Alfa or 4-Factor PCC (50 U/kg)
Dabigatran Toxicity Idarucizumab 5g IV (2x 2.5g vials)
